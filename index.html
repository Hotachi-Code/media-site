Media Publisher

Media Publisher is a personal, single-user tool that helps its owner prepare and publish their own content (text, images and videos) to their own accounts on social platforms such as YouTube, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Threads, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.

How it works

  1. The owner provides a topic, text, image or video.
  2. The tool drafts platform-specific versions of the post.
  3. The owner reviews and approves each post.
  4. The approved post is published to the owner's own account through the platform's official API.

The tool runs on the owner's own computer. It is not a public service, has no other users, and does not access, collect or sell data of third parties.

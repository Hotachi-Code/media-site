Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09

1. Who we are

Media Publisher ("the App") is a personal tool operated by a single individual (the "Owner"). The App is used only by the Owner to publish the Owner's own content to the Owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. Contact: szakalyoli@gmail.com.

2. What data the App handles

  • Authorization tokens (OAuth access and refresh tokens) issued by the platforms the Owner connects. They are stored only on the Owner's own computer and are never shared with anyone.
  • Content the Owner creates (text, images, videos) and basic publishing results (post IDs, status, timestamps), stored locally.
  • Basic profile identifiers of the Owner's own accounts (such as account or channel ID), needed to publish.

The App does not collect, store or process personal data of any other person, does not track visitors, and does not use cookies or analytics.

3. How the data is used

Data is used only to publish the Owner's content to the Owner's own accounts through the official APIs of the connected platforms. It is not sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.

4. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the Owner's own YouTube channel. By using the App, the Owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information handled through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy. The Owner can revoke the App's access to their Google account at any time through the Google security settings page.

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Other platforms

The App may connect to LinkedIn, Pinterest, Meta (Instagram, Facebook, Threads), TikTok and similar services. For each, the App requests only the permissions needed to publish content to the Owner's own account. Use of those services is subject to each platform's own terms and privacy policy. The Owner can revoke the App's access at any time in the settings of the respective platform.

6. Storage, retention and deletion

All data is stored locally on the Owner's computer. The Owner can delete tokens and stored content at any time by deleting the App's local data, and can revoke access on each platform as described above. To request deletion of any data related to the App, contact szakalyoli@gmail.com.

7. Security

Tokens and credentials are kept in local configuration files that are excluded from version control and are not transmitted anywhere except to the respective platform's official API over HTTPS.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data of children.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date above shows the latest revision.
